# DigiLocal Vendor — Privacy Policy

**Effective Date**: 10/08/2026  
**Last Updated**: 18/08/2026  

This Privacy Policy explains how **Zordial Technologies Private Limited**, operating the DigiLocal platform (“DigiLocal”, “we”, “us” or “our”), collects, uses, stores and protects information when you use the DigiLocal Vendor application (`com.digilocal.vendor`) and related services.

DigiLocal Vendor is a merchant platform that enables local shops and suppliers operating within or servicing residential societies and gated communities to manage their stores, products, orders, customers and payouts.

By registering for or using DigiLocal Vendor, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Vendor Personal Information
We may collect:
- Full name
- Mobile number
- Email address
- Account credentials
- OTP authentication information
- Profile information
- Vendor account and verification information

### 1.2 Business Information
We may collect:
- Store/business name
- Shop address
- Business category
- Residential society name and Society ID
- Store logo
- Product catalogue
- Product images
- Business operating information

### 1.3 Government and Tax Information
Where applicable, we may collect:
- GSTIN
- PAN
- GST-related information
- Other information reasonably required for business verification, taxation or legal compliance

### 1.4 Banking and Payout Information
For vendor settlements, we may collect:
- Account holder name
- Bank account number
- IFSC code
- UPI ID
- Payout and settlement information
- Transaction references

*Bank and payment information is used for legitimate payment and settlement purposes and may be shared with relevant payment, banking and settlement service providers.*

---

## 2. Customer Information Accessible to Vendors
When a customer places an order through DigiLocal, a vendor may receive information necessary to fulfil that order, which may include:
- Customer name
- Customer phone number
- Tower/block information
- Flat/unit number
- Delivery information
- Ordered products
- Quantity and unit
- Order value
- Order status

### ⚠️ Vendor Data-Use Restrictions
Customer information provided to a vendor is strictly for fulfilling and supporting the relevant DigiLocal order.

**Vendors must NOT:**
1. Copy or permanently store customer information unnecessarily;
2. Sell or share customer information;
3. Use customer phone numbers for personal marketing;
4. Send unsolicited promotional messages;
5. Contact customers for unrelated business purposes;
6. Use flat/unit information for unauthorized purposes;
7. Export customer information from the Platform;
8. Share customer information with unauthorized persons.

*Any misuse of customer information may result in account suspension or termination and may also lead to legal action where applicable.*

## 3. Device Permissions
Depending on the features enabled in the application, DigiLocal Vendor may request access to:

- **Notifications**:
  - New order alerts
  - Order status updates
  - Payment notifications
  - Important account notifications
- **Alarm / Background Notification Features**:
  - Certain Android permissions may be used to provide high-priority order alerts, including sound notifications or other permitted alert mechanisms when the application is running in the background.
- **Microphone**:
  - Microphone access may be used for voice-based product search and hands-free interactions.
  - Audio may be processed to convert speech into text. We do not intend to permanently store voice recordings for unrelated purposes.
- **Camera and Photos**:
  - Upload store logos
  - Upload product photographs
  - Upload banners
  - Submit photographs required for support or verification
- **Technical Information**:
  - IP address, device information, app version, device identifiers where permitted, Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token, network status, crash and diagnostic information, and security and authentication logs.

## 4. How We Use Information
We may use collected information to:
- Create and manage vendor accounts;
- Verify vendors and businesses;
- Authenticate vendors using OTP or other security mechanisms;
- Register vendors with participating societies;
- Manage product catalogues;
- Process customer orders;
- Send order notifications;
- Facilitate deliveries;
- Calculate commissions and settlements;
- Process T+1 payouts;
- Provide customer and vendor support;
- Detect fraud and unauthorized activity;
- Maintain Platform security;
- Improve DigiLocal services;
- Communicate important service information;
- Comply with applicable laws and regulatory requirements.
